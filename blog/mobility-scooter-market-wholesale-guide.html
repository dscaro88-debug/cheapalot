Mobility Scooters: The Ageing-Population Market Importers Should Not Skip

By CheapALot Team · August 25, 2026 · 8 min read · Electric Bikes & Mobility

While e-bikes grab the headlines, a quieter and very durable segment is growing underneath: electric mobility scooters for older adults. Ageing populations across Europe, North America, Japan and Australia are creating steady, non-seasonal demand - and because buyers are distributors and senior-care retailers, it is a segment that rewards reliability and service over speed and style. Here is why it deserves a place in your range, and how to source it right.

Why This Market Is Different

Mobility scooters are not "another e-bike". Three things set them apart:

  • Demographics, not fads - the over-65 population is growing in every developed market. Demand compounds steadily rather than spiking and crashing.
  • Distributor-driven - typical buyers are medical-equipment distributors, pharmacy chains, senior-living retailers and care networks, not end consumers.
  • Service matters more - buyers need spare parts, batteries, chargers and service manuals. Suppliers who provide them win long-term accounts.

Compliance by Market

Mobility scooters are regulated as assistive devices in most markets, which is actually simpler than e-bike vehicle rules - but the paperwork is still mandatory:

  • Europe - CE marking and MDR conformity as a medical device in many cases.
  • USA - typically FDA Class I (exempt) with UL-certified batteries.
  • Australia - TGA regulation; batteries must meet dangerous-goods rules.
  • Japan - strict standards for motorised mobility devices.

Certification detail and the battery-shipping rules are covered in our guides: E-Bike Certifications Explained and Lithium Battery Shipping.

After-Sales Is Your Moat

Unlike a fashion e-bike, a mobility scooter is a daily necessity for its user. When it breaks, the distributor needs parts fast. This is where most Chinese suppliers fail and where you can win:

  • Order 1-2% of units as spare parts in your first container.
  • Get battery, charger and controller spares in stock.
  • Require service manuals and parts lists in the packing documentation.
  • Establish a clear warranty channel with the factory before you sell the first unit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do mobility scooters need a driving licence?

In most markets, mobility scooters for older adults are classified as assistive devices rather than vehicles, so no licence is required - but local rules vary by country and speed class, and must be checked for each market.

What is the MOQ for mobility scooters?

Trial orders from 20-50 units are typical. OEM colours and decals start around 50 units, with spare parts and service manuals included in the package.

Which mobility scooter market is growing fastest?

Europe and North America lead on volume, driven by ageing populations and reimbursement/insurance schemes. Japan and Australia are smaller but high-value markets.

Why do after-sales support and spare parts matter for mobility scooters?

A mobility scooter is a daily mobility aid - downtime is serious for the user and the retailer's reputation. Distributors who can supply parts and honour warranties win repeat accounts; those who cannot lose them fast.

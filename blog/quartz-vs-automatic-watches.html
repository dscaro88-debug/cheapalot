Quartz vs Automatic Watches: Which Movement to Buy Wholesale?

By CheapALot Team · August 18, 2026 · 6 min read

When ordering watches wholesale, one of the most important decisions is choosing between quartz and automatic movements. This single choice affects your cost, your target market, your after-sales burden, and your profit margins. In this guide, we break down the differences with wholesale-specific insights to help you make the right buying decision.

Quartz Movements: The Volume Seller

Quartz movements are battery-powered and electronically regulated. A small quartz crystal vibrates at 32,768 Hz, providing extremely accurate timekeeping. In the wholesale market, quartz is king for volume sales.

Advantages for Wholesale Buyers

  • Lowest cost: Quartz movements cost $1-3 to manufacture, making complete watches available from $8 wholesale. Our DS-B006 Dress Minimalist at $8 is a prime example.
  • Extreme accuracy: ±15 seconds per month — far more accurate than any mechanical movement.
  • Zero maintenance: Battery replacement every 18-36 months is the only upkeep. No servicing needed.
  • Thin profiles: Quartz movements are 3-5mm thick, allowing slim, fashionable designs.
  • High reliability: Almost no failure rate. Return/warranty rates under 1%.

Disadvantages

  • Lower perceived value: Watch enthusiasts consider quartz "less prestigious."
  • Battery dependency: Dead batteries require customer action — a minor after-sales burden.
  • Lower price ceiling: Hard to sell quartz watches above $100 retail.

Automatic Movements: The Margin Maker

Automatic (self-winding) movements are mechanical. A rotor swings as the wearer moves, winding the mainspring. No battery needed — the watch runs purely on mechanical energy. Automatic watches command higher prices and appeal to a different buyer.

Advantages for Wholesale Buyers

  • Higher margins: Wholesale prices of $18-$42 allow retail pricing of $80-$200+. Our DS-W005 Automatic Skeleton at $42 wholesale can retail for $180+.
  • Premium positioning: "Automatic" on the dial signals quality and craftsmanship.
  • No battery: Self-winding means no battery replacement — set and forget (as long as it's worn).
  • Skeleton displays: Automatic movements can be displayed through transparent casebacks, showing the mechanical work. See DS-B007 Skeleton Automatic.
  • Gift appeal: Mechanical watches feel more "special" as gifts, especially for milestones.

Disadvantages

  • Higher cost: 2-5x more expensive than quartz equivalents.
  • Lower accuracy: ±20-30 seconds per day, vs ±15 seconds per month for quartz.
  • Servicing needed: Every 3-5 years, automatic movements need cleaning and oiling ($30-50 per service).
  • Thicker cases: Automatic movements are 8-12mm thick, limiting slim design options.
  • Higher return rate: 2-3% of automatic watches may need adjustment or repair within the first year.

Choose Quartz If You Sell To:

  • Market stalls and flea markets
  • Online marketplaces (eBay, Amazon, Etsy)
  • Gift shops and tourist shops

  • Wedding and anniversary gift markets

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is better for wholesale: quartz or automatic watches?

It depends on your target market. Quartz watches are better for high-volume, low-price retail (gift shops, market stalls, online marketplaces) due to low cost ($8-15) and zero maintenance. Automatic watches suit premium positioning (jewelry stores, boutique retailers) with higher margins and prices of $18-42 wholesale. A balanced wholesale order should be 60-70% quartz and 30-40% automatic.

How long do quartz watch batteries last?

Quartz watch batteries typically last 18-36 months depending on the movement and usage. Replacement batteries cost $0.50-$2 wholesale and can be replaced by any jeweler in 5 minutes.

Do automatic watches need winding?

Automatic watches self-wind through wrist motion during daily wear. If worn daily for 8+ hours, they maintain power indefinitely. If not worn for 24-48 hours, they stop and need manual winding (20-30 turns of the crown) to restart.

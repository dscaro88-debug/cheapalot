How Much Does a Yiwu Sourcing Agent Cost? Fees, MOQ & When You Need One

By CheapALot Team · August 24, 2026 · 7 min read

You've heard Yiwu is where the world's cheapest wholesale goods come from. But if you're not standing in the market yourself, the practical question is: what does it cost to have someone on the ground source for you?

The honest answer: a Yiwu sourcing agent typically costs 3–8% commission on order value, or a per-item fee of $0.05–0.50 depending on category. For most buyers, that cost is repaid many times over — because an agent gets factory pricing, verifies stock and handles QC that would otherwise cost you a plane ticket and weeks of your time.

How Yiwu Sourcing Agents Charge

There are four common pricing models. Which one applies depends on the agent and the size of your orders:

1. Percentage Commission (Most Common)

Typically 3–8% of the total order value. Lower percentages apply to high-value or repeat orders; higher percentages for small, complex or one-off sourcing jobs. For clearance and bulk stock with larger order values, commissions tend to sit at the lower end — often 3–5%.

2. Per-Item Fee

Common for simple, repeatable products: $0.02–0.50 per unit depending on item value. Works well when you source the same SKU repeatedly and prices are stable.

3. Flat Fee / Retainer

For buyers who need ongoing support — checking stock, sending photos, negotiating monthly — some agents charge a flat fee of $200–1,000/month instead of per-order commission. This suits buyers with multiple active orders.

4. Factory-Direct Markup

Some agents buy from factories themselves and resell to you at a markup (often 10–25% over factory price). This is how many "wholesale suppliers" you find online actually work. It's not necessarily worse — you get a simpler single invoice — but you should know the difference between an agent (your side) and a trader (their own margin).

What a Yiwu Sourcing Agent Actually Does

Before deciding if the cost is worth it, understand what you're paying for:

  • Supplier verification — checking business registration, market booth address and operating history (many factories in Yiwu have been at the same booth for 10+ years — a strong trust signal).
  • Pricing — agents get factory or near-factory pricing; walk-in buyers are often quoted higher "guest prices".
  • Product sourcing across categories — one agent can assemble a mixed container from multiple factories so you hit minimums without over-buying one line.
  • Quality control — pre-shipment inspection, photos, counts and condition checks. For clearance stock, this is the difference between a great deal and a write-off.
  • Consolidation & export — packing, export documentation, arranging freight.

MOQ: What Minimums to Expect

Minimum order quantities through a sourcing agent are more flexible than buying direct from factories, because an agent can consolidate:

  • Direct factory MOQ: often 500–2,000 units per SKU.
  • Via sourcing agent (new production): often 100–300 units per SKU, or as low as 1–2 cartons.
  • Via sourcing agent (clearance stock): frequently as low as a mixed-carton minimum — in our case £250 or a set number of cartons, mix-and-match across categories.

If you're testing a market, clearance stock is the cheapest way in: you can buy small mixed volumes first, learn what sells, then scale with production orders.

When You Don't Need an Agent

Be honest about your situation. You probably don't need an agent if you:

  • Already have a trusted factory relationship and visit Yiwu regularly.
  • Only buy one product line with a stable SKU and handle your own QC.
  • Order full containers of a single item directly from a verified factory.

If any of those apply, buy direct and keep the margin. If you're buying multiple categories, can't visit, or need verified clearance stock, an agent usually pays for itself.

Does an Agent Actually Save You Money?

Run the numbers on a typical $10,000 mixed order: a 5% commission costs $500. Against that, a local agent typically saves you 5–15% on pricing vs walking into the market cold, plus catches QC issues that would cost far more than $500 if they slipped through. The math almost always works — provided the agent is genuinely local, transparent about their fee and shows you real stock before you pay.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a Yiwu sourcing agent charge?

Most Yiwu sourcing agents charge a commission of 3-8% of total order value, or a per-item fee of 0.5-5% depending on category and volume. Some charge a flat monthly retainer. For clearance and bulk stock, commission typically sits at the lower end because order values are higher.

What is the minimum order for a sourcing agent?

Many agents work with minimums around $500-2,000 per order, or 1-2 cartons per line. For clearance stock, mix-and-match across categories helps reach minimums without over-committing. CheapALot's clearance minimum is £250.

When should I use a sourcing agent vs buying direct?

Use an agent when you can't visit the market, need multiple categories in one container, need quality checks before shipping, or want to avoid paying 'foreigner prices' at market booths. Buy direct when you have an established factory relationship and visit regularly.

Can a sourcing agent negotiate better prices than I can?

Usually yes. A local agent with regular relationships gets factory or near-factory pricing, while walk-in buyers are often quoted higher 'guest prices'. Agents also verify stock, handle QC and consolidate shipments, which usually more than covers their commission.
